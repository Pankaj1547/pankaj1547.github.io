KidTube Studio

A personal command-line tool that renders videos on my own computer and uploads them to my own YouTube channels.

What it is

KidTube Studio is a private automation tool. It generates two kinds of video and publishes them on a schedule:

  • Hindi nursery rhymes and children's songs, animated from a local rhyme bank.
  • Generative physics simulations — marble races, falling sand, plinko drops, hydraulic-press crushes and territory-growth simulations.

Everything is rendered locally with ffmpeg, numpy and Pillow. Nothing is scraped, reposted or taken from anyone else.

Who can use it

It is used by one person — its author — to manage their own YouTube channels. It is not a product, it is not offered to other people, and it has no sign-up. There is no server: it runs on a single desktop computer.

What it connects to

It uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and tags, and the YouTube Analytics API to read view and retention figures for those same videos. It signs in as the channel owner through Google OAuth, and it only ever touches that one account.

Privacy

The tool collects no data from anyone. See the privacy policy for the detail.
